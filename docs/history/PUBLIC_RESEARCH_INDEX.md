# Public Seraph research: a dated bridge to this community project

**Public educational bibliography | 24 October 2026 | Read-only sources, not a release certificate**

The Age of Free Programming is an **independent, community-oriented learning architecture** inspired by the publicly explained problems Seraph researches. It is not Seraph Core, a private Mesh export, an installed operating system, a licensed executable, or a claim that Seraph 1.0 software was released. Its users own their own work subject to licenses of the materials and tools they use.

## How to read older Seraph material

1. **History is not current software status.** A June or August document describes its own dated context. Later evidence may revise a conclusion, but does not rewrite the earlier record.
2. **A source is not a test receipt.** Public research can motivate a learner experiment without proving that the community implementation works.
3. **"Public-safe" is not access permission or a code license.** Only link to sources verified available for broad reading; do not copy diagrams, full chapters, internal names, code or research from Google Docs simply because a document is visible.
4. **Claims have different levels:** documented principle, past bounded observation, estimate, research hypothesis, proposed community exercise, independently reproduced result. Keep those labels close to the claims.
5. **One repo is not the entire program.** Seraph, its PLC continuity research, Opus II and specific scientific experiments have distinct scopes. Do not relabel a PLC hypothesis as a shipped feature of this repo.

The source links below were checked for Google Drive's `anyone:reader` permission on **24 October 2026**. Permissions may change. The source title, public access and publication language do not by themselves grant redistribution, trademark rights, derivative-work rights or a software license. Link and describe; do not republish underlying text.

## Publication and source boundary

Private Drive working papers, historical backups, sealed reviews, proprietary schemas, actual Seraph Mesh routes/identifiers and protected operations are **not** sources to import into a public GitHub branch. Nothing here imports their contents. Do not infer that the older Seraph Core PR reviews or a historical four-model study approve v0.4 or any newer master prompt.

**Current product truth:** [Verification status](../VERIFICATION_STATUS.md) governs this repo's own test claims; [privacy/IP/release policy](../PRIVACY_IP_AND_RELEASE_GATE.md) governs public exposure; [sharing and license status](../SHARING_AND_LICENSE_STATUS.md) remains unresolved for general redistribution and executable release.

**Teaching principle:** preserve what changed, what remains unverified, and what must be proved next.
